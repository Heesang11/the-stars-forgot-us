The Stars Forgot Us

A story of love, fate, and second chances.

Supernatural Romance • Fantasy • Mystery

Characters Scene Index The Script Ending

I. Characters

1. Mira — Female Lead

Competitive, observant, and stubborn. Resents Eli after he humiliates her during an academic competition.

2. Eli — Male Lead

Intelligent, reserved, and unintentionally awkward. Beneath his competitive nature, he's thoughtful and kind.

3. Bianca — Mira's Best Friend

Playful, teasing, and supportive. Notices Mira's feelings before Mira admits them to herself.

4. Celeste — The Researcher

Curious and determined. Discovers the old book and investigates the original couple's history.

5. Dani — The Skeptic

Practical and outspoken. Dismisses supernatural explanations until the evidence becomes undeniable.

6. Lila — The Secret-Keeper

Quiet and cautious. Knows her family is connected to the old feud and struggles to reveal the truth.

7. Noah — Eli's Best Friend

Friendly and teasing. Knows Eli's softer side and initially warns him against getting involved with Mira.

8. Iris — Cupid in Disguise

Appears to be an ordinary girl whom Mira once helped. Secretly connects Mira and Eli through an invisible red string, hoping to reunite two souls across lifetimes.

III. The Script

SCENE 1: The Wrong Answer

Location: School hall / classroom

The film opens with a school academic competition attended by students and classmates.

Mira confidently answers a question, but Eli publicly corrects her answer and wins the round.

The audience reacts, and Mira feels humiliated. She gathers her things and leaves, avoiding Eli's gaze.

Eli notices her reaction but doesn't approach her.

Bianca comforts Mira while Noah congratulates Eli.

Later, Mira helps Iris, an apparently ordinary girl, pick up some scattered belongings.

Iris notices Mira's lingering frustration toward Eli and watches the two rivals from a distance.

Purpose: Establish the rivalry.

SCENE 2: Cupid's Little Intervention

Location: School courtyard

Mira and Eli pass each other in the courtyard, exchanging an irritated glance.

Neither notices Iris watching from nearby.

Once they're apart, Iris raises her hand and mysteriously ties an invisible red string around their little fingers.

For a brief moment, the string glows faintly in the sunlight before disappearing.

Iris smiles knowingly and walks away.

Purpose: Introduce the supernatural connection.

SCENE 3: The Unwanted Partnership

Location: Classroom

The literature teacher assigns a project requiring students to explore an unusual historical or literary story.

Mira and Eli are paired together.

Mira looks displeased, while Eli appears surprised but accepts the assignment.

Bianca notices Mira's reaction and teases her afterward.

Noah encourages Eli to take the project seriously, though he notices Eli glancing toward Mira.

Mira and Eli reluctantly agree to search the library for an original topic.

Purpose: Force the rivals to spend time together.

SCENE 4: The Book That Remembers

Location: School library

Mira and Eli search through old books, disagreeing over which topic would make their project stand out.

Celeste, searching nearby, discovers a dusty book containing handwritten letters, faded photographs, and an incomplete account of two forbidden lovers.

The group gathers around the discovery.

One photograph shows a young couple whose faces look exactly like Mira and Eli.

Everyone is startled. Dani suspects a coincidence, while Bianca jokingly suggests reincarnation.

Mira immediately dismisses the possibility and refuses to entertain the idea of being connected to Eli in any way.

Celeste convinces the group to investigate the book further for their project.

Purpose: Introduce the mystery and the original lovers.

SCENE 5: Little Things Change

Location: Classroom / hallway / library

A short montage shows Mira and Eli slowly growing closer while working on their project.

  • Eli saves Mira a seat without mentioning it.
  • Mira notices Eli has remembered which notes belong to her and quietly returns a pen he left behind.
  • They reach for the same book and accidentally touch hands, both quickly withdrawing.
  • Mira catches Eli smiling at one of her silly mistakes.
  • Eli helps Mira organize their materials after she accidentally drops them.
  • Bianca catches Mira watching Eli and teases her.
  • Noah notices Eli keeping a small paper star Mira made during their project.

Mira begins noticing that Eli isn't as arrogant or insensitive as she first believed.

Meanwhile, Celeste researches the original couple, Dani searches for evidence, and Lila becomes increasingly uncomfortable whenever the old families are mentioned.

Purpose: Develop the romance and show minor conflicts.

SCENE 6: The Missing Page

Location: Library

Mira finishes reading the old book and discovers that its final page has been torn out.

The missing ending leaves the original couple's fate uncertain.

Mira returns to the library alone to search for the page.

Eli is already there, holding the missing page.

He reveals that he found it tucked inside another old book while researching their project.

Mira is surprised that he continued investigating on his own.

Their earlier hostility gives way to a more serious conversation about the legend and reincarnation.

Eli appears genuinely fascinated by the idea that two souls might find each other in different lifetimes. Mira, despite her skepticism, begins to wonder about their strange connection.

Purpose: Bring the leads closer and prepare for the major reveal.

SCENE 7: The Curse Was a Lie

Location: Library / empty classroom

Eli shows Mira the missing page and the evidence he found in the old records.

Lila finally admits that her family knows part of the original story.

Celeste pieces together the letters and family histories.

The original lovers belonged to two families who opposed their relationship.

Their families fabricated the curse to keep the lovers apart and preserve an old feud.

The group realizes that the supernatural legend was built around a real act of separation.

Mira and Eli recognize that their own families are connected to the same history.

Mira becomes frightened by the possibility that their relationship could repeat the tragedy, while Eli feels an inexplicable familiarity with the original couple.

Purpose: Reveal that the curse was fabricated by people.

SCENE 8: Across Every Lifetime

Location: Various school locations

Mira and Eli look at the original photograph together.

As they study it, the film transitions into a sequence of dreamlike images representing their past lives.

Different versions of the two meet in different eras and places.

  • In one lifetime, they meet as strangers and become friends.
  • In another, they begin as rivals, constantly challenging one another.
  • In another, they share quiet, tender moments before their families separate them.

Each lifetime ends with the pair being pulled apart by conflict, misunderstanding, or circumstances beyond their control.

The montage returns to the present, where Mira and Eli are standing beside each other, visibly emotional.

They begin to understand that the photograph and their strange familiarity may represent souls that have repeatedly found each other.

Purpose: Show the emotional supernatural reveal.

SCENE 9: A Different Choice

Location: Old classroom

Mira and Eli sit together with the letters and photograph between them.

Eli reveals that his feelings for Mira have grown beyond their original rivalry.

Mira, after a moment of hesitation, acknowledges that her feelings have changed too.

Rather than making a dramatic declaration, they share a quiet moment of mutual understanding.

Mira returns the little paper star Eli kept from their project, then lets him keep it.

They decide that their past lives and family histories should not determine what they do in the present.

Purpose: Resolve the emotional conflict through choice rather than fate alone.

SCENE 10: A Different Ending

Location: Classroom / courtyard

Mira and Eli complete their literature project by exposing the truth behind the fabricated curse.

Their friends help organize the letters, photograph, and historical evidence for their presentation.

The original photograph changes one final time: the lovers who once stood apart now appear together, peaceful and smiling.

Mira and Eli present the story as one of love, prejudice, and the freedom to choose one's future.

Afterward, they walk into the courtyard together, no longer hiding their closeness.

Bianca notices their linked hands and excitedly nudges the others.

Purpose: Break the cycle and give the characters their happy ending.

SCENE 11: The Stars Remember

Location: School courtyard

Iris watches Mira and Eli from a distance as they leave with their friends.

She looks down at her hand, where a faint red glow briefly appears.

For an instant, the invisible string between Mira and Eli becomes visible in the sunlight.

Iris's ordinary appearance gives way to a subtle, otherworldly presence, hinting at her true identity as Cupid.

She watches the couple walk away together, satisfied that the two souls have finally reached a different ending.

The camera lingers on the old photograph, now showing the original lovers together.

Purpose: Reveal Iris's identity and close the supernatural story.

The End

"No matter how many lifetimes pass or how many obstacles separate two souls, they may find each other again, but a happy ending comes when they choose one another."
